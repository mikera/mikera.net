## Rationale

A fair price is the market price in an open market, *after* those who impose
costs on others have compensated for them. Carbon taxes, congestion charges and
pollution levies are not punishments — they are the golden rule applied to
economics. You pay for what you damage.

## The policy

- Price externalities at their true social cost (carbon, pollution, congestion).
- Apply border adjustments so nations cannot gain a trade advantage by exporting
  their externalities to the rest of the world.
