## Rationale

Land, spectrum and the atmosphere's capacity to absorb emissions are not
products of anyone's labour. Their value is created by the community as a whole,
so it should be returned to the community as a whole. Taxing this value is the
one form of taxation that distorts no productive activity — you cannot hide land,
move it offshore, or stop producing it.

## The policy

- An annual levy on the unimproved value of land, replacing taxes that fall on
  labour, trade and enterprise.
- Extend the same principle to other scarce commons (spectrum, extraction rights,
  emissions capacity).
