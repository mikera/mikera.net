## Rationale

If the value of scarce resources belongs to everyone, then that value must be
returned to everyone. This is not welfare and not charity — it is a rightful
return on common ownership. It guarantees a floor beneath every individual,
making the protections of the old economy (means-tested benefits, minimum wage)
unnecessary.

## The policy

- Revenue from land value and externality levies is distributed to every citizen
  as an equal, unconditional dividend.
- No means testing, no conditions, no bureaucracy of eligibility.
